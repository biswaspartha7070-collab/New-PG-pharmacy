Helpful information

Frequently asked questions.

Can I enquire about a medicine before visiting?

Yes. Use the phone or WhatsApp contact option once the pharmacy's number is added. Availability should be confirmed directly with the pharmacy.

Can I order prescription medicines?

Please contact the pharmacy directly. Prescription medicines are supplied only in accordance with applicable laws and a valid prescription where required.

How do I get directions?

Use the Google Maps section or the Directions link. Confirm the correct P G Pharmacy listing before starting your journey.

Can I book an appointment on WhatsApp?

The WhatsApp button can be used for appointment requests or general enquiries after the correct phone number is configured. Appointment availability must be confirmed by the pharmacy.
